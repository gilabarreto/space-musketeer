Space Musketeer


Space Musketeer is a retro-inspired space game where you play as an ambitious Musketeer on a mission to colonize Mars. But the planet is already home to hostile aliens known as Tweeters, who aren't exactly thrilled about the newcomers. Pilot your spacecraft, the EagleY, defeat the resistance, and clear the way for the future Martian colony.
